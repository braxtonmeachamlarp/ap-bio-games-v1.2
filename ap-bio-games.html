Build-an-Atom

An AP Biology review game — Atomic Structure

  • Each round gives you a target atom (or isotope) to build.
  • Use the +/- steppers to add protons and neutrons to the nucleus.
  • Add electrons one at a time — they'll automatically fill the innermost open shell first, just like real atoms.
  • Hit "Check Atom" when you think you've matched the target. Fix any mismatches and try again.
  • After 8 rounds, a bonus concept quiz tests what you learned.
